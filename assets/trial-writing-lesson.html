03 · THE FOUR QUESTIONS04 / 13
BODY 追问四连

观点写完，先别急着换词。

01

为什么？

解释背后的原因或机制。

02

举例呢？

把抽象观点放进具体人物或场景。

03

和谁比？

通过对比让变化更清楚。

04

结果是什么？

说明最终影响，不重复观点。

四个问题是诊断清单，不是要求每一段机械写四句；根据观点选择最能推动逻辑的 2–4 个。

04 · LOCK THE PARAGRAPH JOB05 / 13
先锁定：这一段负责什么？
In some countries young people are encouraged to work or travel for a year between finishing high school and starting university studies.Do you think the advantages outweigh the disadvantages?
Question Type

Advantages outweigh disadvantages

Position

Yes — the advantages are greater.

Body Job

完整解释一个主要优势。

本节只练一个主体段。完整文章仍需处理另一侧，不能只写优势。
05 · START WITH ONE CLEAR IDEA06 / 13
一句话观点

先写清楚，再往下追问。

A gap year can help young people make better university choices.
5.5 常见停法

This is beneficial and can help them in the future.

继续追问

为什么它能改善大学选择？具体经历如何产生作用？

06 · BUILD THE ARGUMENT CHAIN07 / 13
一句观点 → 一条论证链

每一句，都回答前一句留下的问题。

WHY?

为什么选择更好？

工作或独立旅行让学生接触真实责任与不同生活方式。

EXAMPLE

什么场景？

例如在公司工作，或独立安排旅行预算与行程。

COMPARE

和谁比？

相比高中毕业后立即入学，他们更了解自己的兴趣与能力。

RESULT

结果是什么？

更可能选到合适专业，也更少中途转专业。

TR：观点得到具体支持｜CC：原因 → 场景 → 对比 → 结果，逻辑持续向前。

08 · FIND → EXPLAIN → PRIORITISE09 / 13
完整诊断：先抓最影响分数的问题
Taking a gap year is very beneficial because young people can learn many useful things. They may work or travel and gain more experience. This can help them in the future. Therefore, the advantages are greater than the disadvantages.
TR

相关，但理由没有充分展开或支持。

CC

有 because / therefore，但逻辑没有真正推进。

LR

beneficial / useful / experience / future 过于宽泛。

GRA

基本能理解，不是本段最优先的问题。

诊断顺序：问题位置 → 为什么扣分 → 最小修改 → 学生复写

09 · REWRITE LIVE10 / 13
现场改写：不是堆句子，而是补逻辑
Before空泛概括

A gap year is beneficial because young people can gain useful experience and prepare for university.

After56 words

Taking a gap year can help school-leavers choose a more suitable university course. By working in a company or travelling independently, they encounter real responsibilities and discover which subjects and careers genuinely interest them. Compared with students who enter university immediately, they may therefore make a better-informed choice and be less likely to change majors later.

观点场景 / 原因对比结果
更接近 6.5 对观点展开与逻辑推进的要求；不能仅凭一个段落承诺整篇作文达到 6.5。
10 · YOUR TURN11 / 13
虚拟互动 2 · 换到劣势也能用

下一句，哪一个真正回答了“为什么”？

A gap year may place financial pressure on some families.
A

This is a serious disadvantage and can be very bad for young people.

B

If young people cannot find paid work, their parents may have to cover a full year of living and travel costs.

选 A 或 B · 停 3 秒
11 · MAKE THE LOGIC VISIBLE12 / 13
答案：B

不是“更严重”，而是说清楚压力怎么产生。

WHY / MECHANISM

If young people cannot find paid work, their parents may have to cover a full year of living and travel costs.

结果：This extra expense may make a gap year inaccessible to students from lower-income families.

观点 → 为什么 → 具体对象 → 结果

不必每段机械回答四个问题；能让逻辑继续推进，就是有效展开。
